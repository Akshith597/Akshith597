## Featured case studies

### Logistics Control Tower
**Where are delivery performance and fulfillment breaking down?**

A logistics analytics workflow spanning Python extraction, DuckDB, a SQL shipment-grain star schema, quality checks, and Power BI / Streamlit reporting. The full synthetic benchmark covers **98,595 shipments**, with **76.5% on-time delivery** and **62.4% OTIF**.

- Aggregates child records before joins to avoid double counting.
- Documents KPI eligibility, reproduction steps, and dashboard QA.
- Connects delivery and fulfillment exceptions to explicit improvement scenarios.

[View case study and code](https://github.com/Akshith597/logistics-control-tower) · [Interactive demo](https://logistics-control-tow.streamlit.app/)

### Meridian Sales Diagnostic
**Why is the Southern region missing its revenue target?**

A sales performance investigation using **SQL, Python, and Excel**, with an interactive dashboard. The analysis validates **7,742 opportunities** and examines target attainment, conversion, discounting, and gross margin.

- Identifies **84.1% target attainment** and a **23.9 percentage-point win-rate gap** versus other regions in the fictional dataset.
- Reconciles revenue to 40 region-quarter target records within $0.01.
- Prioritizes conversion coaching, discount controls, and large-deal reviews.

[View case study and code](https://github.com/Akshith597/meridian_sales_portfolio) · [Interactive dashboard](https://akshith597.github.io/meridian_sales_portfolio/)
