## Featured projects

### Logistics Control Tower
**Where are delivery performance and fulfillment breaking down?**

[![Power BI executive overview for the Logistics Control Tower synthetic benchmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Akshith597/logistics-control-tower/main/images/Executive_Overview.png)](https://github.com/Akshith597/logistics-control-tower)

A logistics case study covering **98,595 synthetic shipments**, from Python extraction and SQL modeling to Power BI dashboards.

- **Finding:** Full-benchmark on-time delivery is **76.5%**, with **62.4% OTIF**.
- **Analysis:** Aggregates child records before shipment joins to prevent double counting; documents KPI eligibility and dashboard QA.
- **Decision support:** Identifies delivery and fulfillment exceptions and evaluates explicit improvement scenarios.

**Tools:** Python · SQL · DuckDB · Power BI · Streamlit

[Read the case study & code](https://github.com/Akshith597/logistics-control-tower) · [Open the live demo](https://logistics-control-tow.streamlit.app/)

### Meridian Sales Diagnostic
**Why is the Southern region missing its revenue target?**

[![Meridian sales performance dashboard showing regional and sales diagnostics](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Akshith597/meridian_sales_portfolio/main/assets/dashboard_overview.jpg)](https://akshith597.github.io/meridian_sales_portfolio/)

A sales investigation across **7,742 validated opportunities**, examining target attainment, conversion, discounting, and gross margin.

- **Finding:** South achieved **84.1% of its revenue target**, with a **23.9 percentage-point win-rate gap** versus other regions.
- **Validation:** Reconciles revenue to all **40 region-quarter target records within $0.01**.
- **Recommendations:** Prioritizes conversion coaching, discount controls, and large-deal reviews.

**Tools:** SQL · Python · Excel · Interactive dashboard

[Read the case study & code](https://github.com/Akshith597/meridian_sales_portfolio) · [Open the interactive dashboard](https://akshith597.github.io/meridian_sales_portfolio/)

> Both projects use fictional or synthetic data. Findings and improvement scenarios are portfolio demonstrations, not claims of realized business impact.
